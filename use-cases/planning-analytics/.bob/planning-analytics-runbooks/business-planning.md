# Business planning models

Source basis: user guide sections G04, G15, G18, G28. The workflow below is appliance-authored operational guidance, not verbatim IBM procedure. Resolve R references from the source guide and revalidate product/support facts.

## Workflow

1. Identify the planning decision, owner, process calendar, approved definitions and actuals source.
2. Choose FP&A, workforce, supply-chain, sales, ESG/IT or another scoped domain and capture drivers/units.
3. Separate additive quantities from ratios, currency translations, balances and effective-dated assumptions.
4. Define scenario/input/approval boundaries, reconciliation controls and meaningful adoption outcomes.
5. Deliver an illustrative model and acceptance tests, not a vendor ROI claim or certified financial model.

## Evidence and exit gate
Deliver the requested result with source/version scope, validation evidence, remaining unknowns and the next authorized action. A proposed or offline-tested artifact is not a live deployment.
